## Known deployments cited in the LFDT contribution proposal

The following are referenced in the IBC LFDT proposal as production or ecosystem adopters. Confirm and convert to self-reported entries above where possible:

- Ripple (XRPL), Figure, and Progmat among financial infrastructure providers.
- Fetch.ai, Crypto.com, and Injective among broad ecosystem adopters.
- More than 115 connected public networks, plus additional private networks and consortia.

IBC has been in production for five years, processing more than 50 billion dollars in transaction volume with no loss of user funds on chains running supported, up-to-date releases.
