# Maintainers

This file lists the Maintainers of cosmos/ibc, and the people with merge rights. The roles and their criteria are defined in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) and the Charter; this file records who currently holds them and how the role is granted and removed. Maintainers are the voting members of the TSC.

## Becoming a Maintainer

A Contributor is nominated for Maintainer by an existing Maintainer, on the strength of a sustained record of substantial, reviewed contributions to the project. As a guideline this is a sustained period of consistent activity, on the order of a few months, rather than a fixed count of pull requests, with the TSC setting and applying the bar. Nomination is a pull request adding the person to this file. It passes by majority approval of the TSC, with lazy consensus among Maintainers as the default and a vote where consensus is not reached.

## Stepping down and emeritus

Anyone may step down at any time by a pull request removing themselves. A Maintainer who is inactive for an extended period, as defined in the Charter, may be moved to emeritus by a majority vote of the TSC. Emeritus members keep the project's thanks and can be reinstated on request without repeating the full path, subject to a Maintainer vote.

## Removal

Merge rights may be revoked for cause, including Code of Conduct violations or sustained conduct against the project's interest, by a Maintainer vote under the Charter. There is no individual veto; a single objection slows and forces discussion under lazy consensus but does not unilaterally block. Serious cases follow the Code of Conduct process rather than this file.
